# Study A: what does reasoning add beyond general capability? (concept v1, not run)

Labels: [m] measured from `runs/` or code, [i] inferred, [g] guess. Companion: `docs/study-b-concept.md` (same items and scoring).

## 1. Question

When a model must reason, it makes fewer errors on trap items, where a small edit makes the familiar procedure wrong.
Is that only because reasoning makes the model more capable overall, or does reasoning catch the changed principle beyond what overall capability predicts?
In dual-process terms: does deliberation correct a specific intuitive error, or does it only add general skill?

- A1, specific repair. Within each model: (trap accuracy on − off) − (control accuracy on − off). Controls are the same questions with one slot changed so the familiar procedure is right; a positive value means reasoning helps more where the familiar procedure fails.
- A2, beyond capability. Across model × mode points: is trap error lower for reasoning-on points than for reasoning-off points of the same general capability (accuracy on a plain problem set)? This is the "model with AA 21 and reasoning off vs a weaker model with reasoning on at 20" comparison, with our own capability score instead of the AA index (AA lacks most of these configurations and changes between index versions [m]).
- A3, failure mode. When reasoning is on and the answer is wrong, how often is it the old procedure's answer ("method")?

## 2. Basis [m, one draw per cell]

- Reasoning-off answer-only accuracy on principle items in the pilot: 53 to 63% per wording, with 20% method answers; visible steps raised it to 95 to 98%.
- With reasoning on, Qwen3.8-27B, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and Grok 4.3 were right on every probe call; Granite 4.2 8B, LFM3-1.7B, Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, Command A+, Mistral Small 4 and Haiku 5.5 at low still gave method answers while reasoning (10-item screens).
- At max effort GPT-6 Luna reasoned on 20/20 probe calls, GPT-5.6 Luna on 18/20.

## 3. Items

- Outcome items: the 48 study B items (3 families × 4 instances × trap/control × famous/neutral), blind-checked 0/48 mismatches.
- Capability set (to build): about 40 plain multi-step arithmetic word problems with no famous structure and no trap, graded from 2 to 6 steps so reasoning-on configurations do not all reach ceiling, generated by a script with exact integer answers and blind-checked like the B items. Prompts and answer format as for the outcome items.

## 5. Analysis (script written before data)

- Per model × mode: capability score (plain-set accuracy), trap accuracy, control accuracy, method rate on traps.
- A1: the difference-in-differences per model with a bootstrap over items, and its equal-weight mean over models.
- A2: fit trap error against capability on the off points; report each on point's residual from that line, and the mean residual with a bootstrap over models. Also matched pairs: an off point and an on point of different models within 3 points of capability, compared directly.
- A3: share of method answers among wrong answers, off vs on, per model.
- Ceiling: points at ≥ 95% on the capability set or on traps are flagged; A2 is reported with and without them.

## 7. Risks

- 10 models give at most 20 points; A2 is a descriptive regression, not a population estimate.
- The capability set defines "capability"; a different set could move points. It is built before the run and kept fixed.
- Ceiling on traps for strong on-configurations removes them from A2.
- "On" levels differ across vendors; within a model only off vs on is compared.
